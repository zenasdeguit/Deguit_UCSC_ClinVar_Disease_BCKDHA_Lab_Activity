### Exon and Intron Explanation

An **exon** is a section of a gene that is retained in the mature RNA transcript, while an **intron** is an intervening region that is removed during RNA processing. In the UCSC gene model, exons are represented by blocks and introns are represented by the lines connecting the exon blocks.

### Relative Intron and Exon Length

The introns generally appear much longer than the exons in the BCKDHA gene. The exon blocks are relatively short, while the connecting intronic regions span much larger genomic distances.
